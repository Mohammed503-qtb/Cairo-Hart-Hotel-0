   134→# 2. PURPOSE OF THE WEBSITE
   135→
   136→The website has five primary jobs:
   137→
   138→## Job 1 — Sell the hotel
   139→
   140→The visitor must understand:
   141→
   142→- What the hotel is.
   143→- Where it is.
   144→- What rooms are available.
   145→- Why they should stay.
   146→- What facilities exist.
   147→- What the price/value proposition is.
   148→
   149→## Job 2 — Reduce booking friction
   150→
   151→The guest should be able to:
   152→
   153→```text
   154→Search → Select → Enter Data → Review → Confirm
   155→```
   156→
   157→without unnecessary steps.
   158→
   159→## Job 3 — Create reliable reservations
   160→
   161→A successful booking must create a real reservation record that can later be consumed by the future PMS/application.
   162→
   163→## Job 4 — Confirm the reservation clearly
   164→
   165→The guest should leave the booking process knowing:
   166→
   167→- Booking number.
   168→- Hotel.
   169→- Room.
   170→- Dates.
   171→- Guests.
   172→- Price.
   173→- Payment state.
   174→- Policies.
   175→- Contact details.
   176→
   177→## Job 5 — Become the public digital front door of the hotel
   178→
   179→The website should remain useful even when a visitor is not ready to book yet.
   180→

   720→# 20. AVAILABILITY
   721→
   722→Availability must be derived from actual inventory.
   723→
   724→The website must consider:
   725→
   726→- Existing confirmed reservations.
   727→- Relevant pending holds.
   728→- Active stays if inventory model requires it.
   729→- Rooms unavailable for sale.
   730→- Room blocks.
   731→- Out-of-order inventory.
   732→- Date overlap.
   733→- Room type capacity.
   734→
   735→## Critical rule
   736→
   737→The website MUST NOT rely only on a front-end calculation to determine final availability.
   738→
   739→Final availability must be checked again at reservation creation.
   740→
   741→---
   742→
   743→# 21. DOUBLE-BOOKING PREVENTION
   744→
   745→The booking process must protect inventory against race conditions.
   746→
   747→Example:
   748→
   749→```text
   750→Guest A sees 1 Deluxe room available
   751→Guest B sees 1 Deluxe room available
   752→Both click Book
   753→```
   754→
   755→The system must ensure that only one reservation receives the same inventory.
   756→
   757→This requires a transactional or otherwise atomic reservation mechanism appropriate to the chosen backend/database.
   758→
   759→Frontend checks are not enough.
   760→

   785→

   828→# 24. PRICE CALCULATION
   829→
   830→Price calculation should follow a deterministic pipeline:
   831→
   832→```text
   833→Base Rate
   834→+
   835→Applicable Extras
   836→+
   837→Applicable Taxes/Fees
   838→-
   839→Discounts
   840→=
   841→Booking Total
   842→```
   843→
   844→The exact tax/fee treatment depends on the hotel's jurisdiction and configuration.
   845→
   846→All amounts must use proper money handling.
   847→
   848→Do not use floating-point arithmetic carelessly for financial values.
